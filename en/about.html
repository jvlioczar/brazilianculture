about the guide

E aí, I’m Julio.

This guide grew out of a wish to introduce Brazil to the world with the same care I take explaining Australia to Brazilians.

Brazilian Culture is a special project of BiblioVerse, created by Julio Cesar Prava.

Julio is a graphic designer and content creator, a Brazilian living in Brisbane. On his profile @gdayjulio he shares tips on studying, working and living in Australia, and funny videos about the country.

Privacy

In short: we don’t collect anything about you.

Brazilian Culture has no sign-ups, forms, ads, cookies or analytics. Your browser stores three small preferences on your own device: the colour theme, your language and whether you’ve seen the opening. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data erases them.

To load the page, your browser also connects to Google Fonts (for the fonts) and biblioverse.org (for the BiblioVerse bar at the top). The Music page also has a Spotify player, loaded from Spotify under its own rules. These services receive the usual technical data of any visit, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Links to other sites, such as Wikipedia and the sources cited, follow those sites’ rules.
